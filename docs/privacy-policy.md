# Privacy Policy — GridVolt: Circuit Puzzle

_Effective date: 4 November 2026_

GridVolt: Circuit Puzzle ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game made by
an independent developer, abderrahmanTBD ("I", "me"). This policy explains
what information the app handles and why.

**In short:** the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it never
asks for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any other personal
information. If the app crashes, an anonymous technical report is sent so I
can fix the bug.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following **only on your device**, so the game can continue
where you left off:

- your high score and the game in progress (board, pieces, score);
- your settings (sound on/off) and whether you have seen the tutorial.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its
storage deletes it.

## Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends a crash report to
[Sentry](https://sentry.io), a service I use to find and fix bugs. A report
contains:

- technical details of the error (the error message and where in the code it
  happened);
- your device model, operating system version, screen size and language
  setting;
- the app version;
- a random identifier for the app installation and the session, used to count
  how many people a crash affects. It is not linked to you or your device's
  advertising ID.

Crash reports **do not** include your name, email address, contacts,
location, photos, screenshots or anything you type. IP addresses are not
stored.

Crash reports are used only to diagnose and fix problems in the app. They are
never sold, shared for advertising or used to identify you. Sentry processes
them on my behalf under its own
[privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/), and they are deleted
automatically within 90 days.

## Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. It uses the internet only to send
crash reports.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone, including children under 13.
